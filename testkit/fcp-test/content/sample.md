# Slow mornings

Some mornings start before we're ready. The alarm rings, the phone lights up, and the day seems to begin without asking us.

A slow morning is not about waking up late. It's about the first few minutes belonging to you. A glass of water before the screen. Opening a window. Sitting for a moment with a warm cup and not deciding anything yet.

People who try it often notice small things: the light on the wall, the sound of the street, how their shoulders feel. None of it needs to be productive.

You don't need an hour. Ten quiet minutes can change how the rest of the morning feels. And if today isn't that kind of morning, tomorrow is another try.
